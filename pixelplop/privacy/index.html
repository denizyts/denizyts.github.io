Pixel Plop – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

  • Pixel Plop has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, age, photos or contacts.
  • We run no servers for the game, and we add no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own and no tracking. Google's ad SDK processes diagnostic and performance data as described in section 3.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalized ads only, and is limited to ads rated G (general audiences).
  • The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • The shop and links that leave the app are behind a grown-up check.

1. Who we are

Pixel Plop is made by Deniz Yetiş, 7501 Sk. No: 1, Karşıyaka, İzmir, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, write to denizyetis56@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

To make the game work, the app saves the following on your device using local storage:

  • best score, collection album progress and unlocked themes;
  • settings: sound, vibration, language, theme and the break reminder;
  • whether “Remove Ads” has been purchased (a copy of the store's answer, so the game also works offline);
  • simple counters used to space out ads, such as the number of rounds played and the time of the last ad.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app, and it does not contain your name or contact details. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), if you have turned it on, may include it under Apple's or Google's terms. A purchased “Remove Ads” can always be recovered with Restore Purchases, because the store keeps the record of the purchase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pixel Plop is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. We configure every ad request as follows, before the first ad is requested:

  • Child-directed treatment (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR) are switched on;
  • only non-personalized ads are requested – ads are not based on your interests or past behavior;
  • the maximum ad content rating is G;
  • on iPhone and iPad the app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA); on Android the app removes the advertising ID (AAID) permission.

To show and count an ad, Google's software in the app connects to Google and may process limited technical information, such as the IP address (used to estimate the country or region), app-scoped device identifiers (such as the IDFV on iOS or the App Set ID on Android, never the advertising ID), device type, operating system version, app version, language, and whether an ad was shown or tapped, as well as technical diagnostic data. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive any information that identifies you.

Ads are never shown in the middle of a round. Videos that give an in-game reward are only shown if you choose to watch them. If you buy “Remove Ads”, the app stops requesting the ads that the purchase removes. Without an internet connection, no ads are loaded and the game still works.

4. In-app purchases

The only purchase is the one-time “Remove Ads”. Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email or payment card details. The app only receives a confirmation from the store that the purchase exists, and keeps it on the device. Purchases are behind a grown-up check.

5. What we do not do

  • no account, login, chat or social features;
  • no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own (Google's ad SDK processes diagnostic and performance data as described in section 3), and no tracking across other apps or websites;
  • no access to location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos or contacts;
  • no push notifications;
  • we do not sell or rent any data.

6. Children's privacy

Pixel Plop can be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or anyone) ourselves. Under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR (including protections for children, such as Article 8 GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the app is designed so that the only data leaving the device is the limited technical information needed by Google to serve non-personalized, child-directed ads described in section 3.

Parents and guardians can contact us at denizyetis56@gmail.com with any questions. Because we hold no data about your child, there is nothing on our side to review or delete; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. For information processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy linked above.

7. Legal bases (GDPR and KVKK)

  • On-device storage is strictly necessary to provide the game you asked to play (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f); ePrivacy rules on strictly necessary storage).
  • Non-personalized ads are based on our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). You can avoid them by purchasing “Remove Ads” or playing offline.
  • Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards (GDPR Chapter V; KVKK Art. 9).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority (for example your EU data protection authority, the UK ICO, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority – KVKK). To exercise a right, contact us at denizyetis56@gmail.com. Since we do not hold your data, we will explain how the information is handled and point you to Google where relevant.

9. How long information is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app. Information processed by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

10. Security

Game data stays on your device. Communication between the app and Google's ad servers and the app stores is encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. The current version is always linked from the app's settings and the store listing.
